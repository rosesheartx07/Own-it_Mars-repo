# Project overview

## Problem
Space-themed STEM content for kids is often too abstract (posters, loose facts) or too technical. Few young players ever see the decision-making behind a mission, where power, air, radiation and communication compete with each other.

## Solution
A top-down game where the player walks around a Martian outpost, does tasks in pop-up windows, and faces timed events. Each option has a probability of a bad consequence. After each decision, the game explains the real science behind the event and credits the source.

## Audience
Kids aged 7–12. It can also be used in a classroom or with family.

## What the player learns
- Energy is limited and has to be shared between systems.
- Storms force you to choose what to protect.
- Radiation is a real risk for people working outside.
- Communication with Earth matters for a mission to succeed.
- A good decision does not always give a good result; that is how engineers and mission planners work.
