# Science and data sources

This version of the game uses only the sources below. They come from NASA / JPL pages and from resources provided by the challenge (including the Canadian Space Agency).

| Source | What it supports | Where in the game | Type |
|---|---|---|---|
| NASA / JPL: large solar storm at Mars (2017) | Aurora 25 times brighter; ground radiation doubled | Solar storm event | Curated text |
| NASA: dust accumulation and cleanings on rover solar panels | Dust reduces sunlight; wind sometimes clears it | Dust storm event, solar panels | Curated text |
| NASA: 10 things about the massive dust storm | Opportunity suspended science; Curiosity runs on a nuclear battery | Dust storm event | Curated text |
| CSA: Spacewalks | Maintenance work and what can fail | Habitat | Curated text |
| CSA: Growing food in space (APH) | Controlling temperature, humidity and lighting | Greenhouse | Curated text |
| CSA: Robotics mission control centre | How a robotics operation works | Robot garage | Curated text |
| CSA: Science experiments in space | Risks of long missions; purpose of lab science | Expedition, laboratory | Curated text |

## Important
- All content above is **curated and archived text**. None of it is live data, and the game never presents it as live.
- The sources are credited inside the game, naming the publisher (NASA / JPL-Caltech or Canadian Space Agency).
- Probabilities, costs and timers are game design values.

## Not used in this version
The challenge also offers live and archived data services (for example DONKI, OSDR, RadLab and Mars Trek). They are not part of this version. See [roadmap.md](roadmap.md).
